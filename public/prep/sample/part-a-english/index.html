ข้อสอบครูผู้ช่วย ภาค ก ภาษาอังกฤษ พร้อมเฉลย

Grammar, Vocabulary, Conversation และ Reading ในแนวข้อสอบ ภาค ก (ความรู้ความสามารถทั่วไป) พร้อมเฉลยและคำอธิบาย ลองตอบในใจก่อน แล้วกด "ดูเฉลย"

ข้อ 1

Choose the most appropriate response to complete the conversation. A: Excuse me, I think there's a mistake on my bill. B: ____

  1. ก. You're welcome to pay.
  2. ข. Yes, it's my mistake bill.
  3. ค. No, the bill is correct paper.
  4. ง. Oh, I'm sorry. Let me check it for you.
ดูเฉลย
ตอบข้อ ง. เมื่อลูกค้าแจ้งว่าบิลผิด พนักงานควรขอโทษและตรวจสอบให้
ข้อ 2

Choose the most appropriate response to complete the conversation. A: Could you turn down the music? I'm trying to study. B: ____

  1. ก. I'm studying music.
  2. ข. Sure, I'll turn it up.
  3. ค. Yes, the music is down.
  4. ง. Oh, sorry. I didn't realize it was so loud.
ดูเฉลย
ตอบข้อ ง. turn down หมายถึงลดเสียง การตอบที่เหมาะสมคือขอโทษและยอมลดเสียง ส่วน turn it up คือเพิ่มเสียง

ข้อ 4

Read the passage and answer the question. Artificial intelligence, or AI, is now used in many areas of daily life. It can recommend videos, translate languages, and even help doctors find diseases in X-ray images. In education, AI tools can give students instant feedback on their writing and create practice exercises that match each learner's level. However, AI also raises concerns. Students may depend on AI to do their homework instead of thinking for themselves, and the information AI provides is not always accurate. Therefore, teachers play an important role in guiding students to use AI responsibly—as a helpful assistant, not as a replacement for their own learning. What is the passage mainly about?

  1. ก. How to build a robot
  2. ข. Why teachers should be replaced by AI
  3. ค. The uses and concerns of AI, especially in education
  4. ง. The history of X-ray machines
ดูเฉลย
ตอบข้อ ค. บทความกล่าวถึงการใช้ AI และข้อกังวล โดยเน้นด้านการศึกษา
ข้อ 5

In the phrase "tuition fees," the word "tuition" refers to ___.

  1. ก. uniforms
  2. ข. transport
  3. ค. meals
  4. ง. teaching

ตอบข้อ ง. tuition แปลว่า การสอน tuition fees จึงหมายถึงค่าเล่าเรียน

ข้อ 7

Read the passage and answer the question. Volunteering is a great way for young people to develop skills and help their communities. When students volunteer, they may teach younger children, clean public places, or help at hospitals and animal shelters. These activities teach responsibility, teamwork, and communication. Volunteering can also help students discover their interests and possible careers. For example, a student who helps at a clinic may decide to become a nurse. Many universities consider volunteer experience when they select new students. Most importantly, volunteering gives young people a sense of purpose and shows them that they can make a positive difference in other people's lives. Which skill is mentioned as being developed through volunteering?

  1. ก. Driving
  2. ข. Teamwork
  3. ค. Cooking
  4. ง. Swimming
ดูเฉลย
ตอบข้อ ข. บทความระบุว่าการอาสาช่วยพัฒนาความรับผิดชอบ การทำงานเป็นทีม และการสื่อสาร

ข้อ 11

Read the passage and answer the question. PARENT-TEACHER MEETING Dear Parents, We would like to invite you to the Parent-Teacher Meeting for the first semester on Saturday, 14 September, from 9:00 a.m. to 11:30 a.m. The meeting will be held in each student's classroom. Homeroom teachers will report on your child's academic progress and behavior and discuss ways to support learning at home. Report cards will be given out at the end of the meeting. If you are unable to attend, please inform the homeroom teacher in advance to arrange another time. Parking is available in the school's sports field. Sincerely, The School Director The phrase 'in advance' means ____.

  1. ก. before the event
  2. ข. during the event
  3. ค. instead of the event
  4. ง. after the event
ดูเฉลย
ตอบข้อ ก. in advance หมายถึงล่วงหน้า
ข้อ 12

Read the passage and answer the question. NOTICE: CHANGES TO BUS SERVICE From 1 July, School Bus Route B will leave the city center at 6:45 a.m. instead of 7:00 a.m. This change is due to heavy morning traffic on Rama Road, which has caused the bus to arrive late several times this term. The afternoon schedule will not change. Students who use Route B are asked to be at the bus stop at least five minutes before departure. Please note that the bus will not wait for late passengers. Parents with questions may contact the Transport Office at 02-123-4567 during office hours. What can be inferred from the notice?

  1. ก. The bus has sometimes arrived late at school this term.
  2. ข. Late students will be driven by their teachers.
  3. ค. The Transport Office is open 24 hours.
  4. ง. The afternoon bus will leave earlier too.

ข้อ 17

Read the passage and answer the question. Getting enough sleep is essential for teenagers. Doctors recommend that people aged 13 to 18 sleep eight to ten hours a night. However, many teenagers sleep much less because of homework, social media, and late-night gaming. Lack of sleep can affect memory, mood, and even physical health. Studies show that students who sleep well tend to get better grades and are less likely to feel stressed. To improve sleep, experts advise teenagers to go to bed at the same time every night, avoid caffeine in the evening, and put away phones at least one hour before bedtime, since the light from screens can make it harder to fall asleep. Why should teenagers put away phones before bed?

  1. ก. Social media is always harmful.
  2. ข. Screen light can make it harder to fall asleep.
  3. ค. Parents need to use the phones.
  4. ง. Phones are too expensive.
ดูเฉลย
ตอบข้อ ข. บทความอธิบายว่าแสงจากหน้าจอทำให้หลับยากขึ้น
